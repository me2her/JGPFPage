Portfolio

Throughout my time at uni so far I've made 3 games along with my friend Luna (and I have ideas for bigger scale games too) and this site shows them off.

Toy Tumble

Toy Tumble is a Foddian platformer about a neglected toy who wishes to leave their owner, the game takes the player from one end of the room to the window by the side of the bed.

Foddian games are a subgenre of Platformers, focussing on precision platforming and a lack of checkpoints, based on "Getting Over It With Bennett Foddy". While Toy Tumble does have checkpoints in easy mode, they aren't too forgiving and one section can still send you all the way back to the start of the game.

Super Hamster Quest

Super Hamster Quest is a dungeon crawler about a hamster trying to get it's food back from rats. The owners of the hamsters are away on holiday and The Giant Rat That Makes All The Rules decides its the best moment to steal all the hamsters seeds.

You play as Hamus and have to fight all the rats to get your seeds back, only once you've gotten most of your seeds back from the normal rats you can fight The Giant Rat Who Makes All The Rules and get the rest of your seeds back.

Foul Fowls

Foul Fowls is a game about giant pigeons. The villain "Robin Banks" has set out giant pigeons that are wreaking havoc across the city and it's your job to collect them all.

The game is split into 3 times of day, with difficulty only getting harder as levels go on and the time limit shortening each time.
